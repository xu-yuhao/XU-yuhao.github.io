The paper “Combustion of n-propylbenzene: Experiments and numerical modeling” by S. Guo, Dr. Xu, and co-authors was recognized as a Distinguished Paper of the Flame Dynamics and Transport Processes Colloquium at the 40th International Symposium of The Combustion Institute in Milan, Italy.
